# Formal Response to Leigha West's Feedback: DowUrk Sports & Leadership Park

**Date:** April 30, 2026
**To:** Leigha West
**From:** DowUrk Inc. Leadership Team
**Subject:** Response to Feedback on DowUrk Sports & Leadership Park Proposal

Dear Leigha,

Thank you for taking the time to review the DowUrk Sports & Leadership Park proposal and for providing such thoughtful, constructive feedback. We deeply appreciate your genuine interest in the project's success and your willingness to share your insights. Your perspective is invaluable as we refine our strategy to better serve the community.

We have carefully reviewed your comments and have outlined our responses and action plans below.

## 1. Funder Readiness and Background Information

**Your Feedback:** You noted that funders would benefit from more background information, including target population, demographics, participants served, statistics about opportunities in the area, and the rationale behind choosing ULM and SLU as partners. You also emphasized the importance of clearly defining the community's need.

**Our Response:** We completely agree. A strong, data-driven narrative is essential for securing funding. We are currently developing a comprehensive **Community Needs Assessment Tool** within the DowUrk AI Hub. This tool will allow us to systematically gather and analyze demographic data, identify specific community needs, and track the impact of our programs. This data will be directly integrated into our grant applications to provide funders with a clear, evidence-based understanding of the population we serve and the critical gaps we are addressing. Regarding our partnerships with ULM and SLU, we will explicitly articulate how their specific academic programs and resources align with our mission to provide comprehensive support to entrepreneurs.

## 2. Mission Alignment and Program Evaluation

**Your Feedback:** You emphasized that the mission statement should be the center of everything and cautioned against the common nonprofit pitfall of discovering a gap between community needs and offerings. You also generously provided materials from a program evaluation class.

**Our Response:** Thank you for sharing those valuable resources. We are committed to ensuring that every initiative we undertake is tightly aligned with our core mission of empowering underrepresented entrepreneurs. The Community Needs Assessment Tool mentioned above will be instrumental in preventing any disconnect between our offerings and the actual needs of the community. We will incorporate the principles of program evaluation you shared to establish robust performance indicators and continuously measure our impact against our mission.

## 3. Cultural Inclusivity and Heritage

**Your Feedback:** You raised a thoughtful question regarding the focus on Italian heritage in Independence and suggested expanding the scope to a more diverse heritage center. You specifically mentioned the local Hungarian settlement museum and the African American Heritage Museum in Tangipahoa Parish as potential partners for field trips or events. You also suggested replacing the word "innovation" with "inclusion."

**Our Response:** This is an excellent point. While acknowledging the specific history of Independence is important, our overarching goal is to foster a truly inclusive environment that celebrates the diverse heritage of all the communities we serve. We will actively explore partnerships with the African American Heritage Museum and other local cultural institutions to create a more comprehensive and representative cultural experience. Furthermore, we strongly agree with your suggestion regarding language. We will update our messaging to prioritize "inclusion" over "innovation," as it more accurately reflects our commitment to creating accessible opportunities for everyone.

## 4. District-Specific Programming and Partnerships

**Your Feedback:** You provided several specific suggestions for different districts, including exploring culinary programs in Mandeville (District 3), contacting LSU Ag for STEM and 4-H activities (District 4), and considering the proximity of zones and existing resources like Northshore STEM (District 5). You also asked about the need for a retreat center and the possibility of offering mental health services.

**Our Response:** Your localized knowledge is incredibly helpful. We are taking the following actions based on your suggestions:
*   **District 3:** We will investigate potential partnerships with local culinary programs. Regarding mental health, we recognize this as a critical need for entrepreneurs. We have recently added a comprehensive "Mental Wellness for Entrepreneurs" guide to our Learning Library and are exploring the feasibility of incorporating sensory rooms or partnering with counseling programs for internships.
*   **District 4:** We will reach out to LSU Ag to explore collaborations on STEM and agricultural initiatives. We are also carefully reviewing the insurance and logistical requirements for equipment rentals and animal-related activities.
*   **District 5:** We will map out existing resources like the Northshore STEM program to ensure our offerings are complementary rather than duplicative. We are also actively working to strengthen our ties with the SLU entrepreneur program.

## 5. PROOF System and Implementation Timeline

**Your Feedback:** You asked if the PROOF system badges could be displayed on LinkedIn and astutely pointed out that the implementation timeline must account for the lengthy grant funding process.

**Our Response:**
*   **LinkedIn Integration:** Yes! We are currently developing the functionality to allow users to seamlessly export and display their verified PROOF badges directly on their LinkedIn profiles. This will significantly enhance the professional value of the credentials they earn through our platform.
*   **Timeline:** We appreciate the reality check regarding grant timelines. We are revising our implementation schedule to reflect a phased rollout, ensuring that we have sustainable operational support at each stage and are not overly reliant on immediate grant funding for core activities.

Leigha, your feedback has directly influenced our strategic planning and the development of new features within the DowUrk platform. We are deeply grateful for your support and your commitment to our shared vision of empowering the community.

Sincerely,

The DowUrk Inc. Leadership Team
