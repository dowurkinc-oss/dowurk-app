## Core Concepts in Louisiana Business Compliance

Staying compliant is not just about avoiding penalties; it is about building a sustainable and reputable business. In Louisiana, compliance involves several key areas, from initial registration to ongoing reporting and tax obligations. Understanding these requirements is the first step towards long-term success.

### The Role of the Louisiana Secretary of State (SOS)

The Louisiana Secretary of State is the primary authority for business filings and records in the state. The SOS website, and its geauxBIZ portal, are indispensable resources for entrepreneurs. From registering your business to filing annual reports, the SOS provides the platform for many of your essential compliance tasks.

### geauxBIZ: Your One-Stop Compliance Portal

The geauxBIZ portal is a digital gateway for Louisiana businesses, designed to streamline the process of registration and compliance. It allows you to create a customized business license checklist, register your business entity, and manage various filings online. For any entrepreneur in Louisiana, becoming familiar with geauxBIZ is a critical step.

## Actionable Steps for Louisiana Business Compliance

To ensure your business remains in good standing, it is crucial to follow a structured approach to compliance. The following steps and tables outline the key requirements and deadlines you need to be aware of.

### 1. Business Registration and Formation

Your compliance journey begins with choosing a business structure and registering it with the state. Whether you opt for an LLC, corporation, or another entity type, you will need to file formation documents with the Louisiana Secretary of State.

| Business Structure | Formation Document | Filing Fee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Limited Liability Company (LLC) | Articles of Organization | $100 |
| Corporation | Articles of Incorporation | $75 |

### 2. Annual Report Filing

All LLCs and corporations in Louisiana are required to file an annual report with the Secretary of State. This report keeps your business information current and is essential for maintaining your good standing.

| Requirement | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Due Date** | On or before the anniversary of your business's formation. |
| **Filing Fee** | $35 for LLCs, $30 for corporations. |
| **Filing Method** | Online through the geauxBIZ portal or by mail. |
| **Required Information** | Company name, physical address, registered agent details, and officer/member information. |

### 3. Tax Obligations

Louisiana businesses are subject to various state taxes. Understanding and meeting these obligations is a critical aspect of compliance.

| Tax Type | Filing Frequency | Key Deadlines |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **State Income Tax** | Annually | May 15th for corporations. |
| **Sales Tax** | Monthly, quarterly, or annually | 20th of the month following the reporting period. |
| **Employer Withholding Tax** | Quarterly | Last day of the month following the end of the quarter. |
| **Franchise Tax** | Annually | 15th day of the 5th month after the close of the taxable year. |

### 4. Licenses, Permits, and Registered Agents

Beyond initial registration, your business may require specific licenses and permits to operate legally. Additionally, all business entities must maintain a registered agent in Louisiana.

| Compliance Area | Key Considerations |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Business Licenses** | Most businesses require an occupational license, which must be renewed annually. Deadlines vary by parish. |
| **Industry-Specific Permits** | Depending on your industry (e.g., food service, construction), you may need additional permits. |
| **Registered Agent** | You must maintain a registered agent with a physical address in Louisiana to receive legal notices. |
